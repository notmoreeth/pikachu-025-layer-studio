This is the stack glued in order: base, shop with holes, side flowers, body, front bed. Pull apart to check that nothing important is hidden.

  1. Buy 16 copies of Pikachu 025/128. One stays whole as the back.
  2. Agree the shapes here first. Drag each blob until it covers the matching part of the art.
  3. Shop layer: cut the holes, keep the flower shop. Two copies.
  4. Body, left flowers, right flowers, front bed: cut the blob, throw away the rest. Two of each, three of the body.
  5. Paint the white core on every cut edge. Glue duplicates face to face. Foam tape only between planes.
  6. Order from the back: whole card, shop, left and right flowers, body, front bed.

Four of the 16 are spares. Do not cut eyes, cheeks, or single petals. The blade cannot hold them on a card this small.
